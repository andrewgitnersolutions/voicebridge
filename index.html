Special Education & Accessibility

Universal Design for Learning (UDL) by Default

VoiceBridge meets strict WCAG AAA guidelines. Whether a student uses an assistive switch, high-contrast display, or open-dyslexic typography, VoiceBridge adapts effortlessly.

  • ✓ Lexend & OpenDyslexic Typography: Clinically proven fonts that reduce visual crowding and character flipping.
  • ✓ WCAG AAA 7:1 High Contrast Theme: High-visibility yellow-on-black for low-vision learners.
  • ✓ 48px+ Touch Targets: Easy interaction for students using touch Chromebooks or motor accommodations.
  • ✓ Single-Key Switch Access: Press Space to record/stop and Esc to cancel.

VoiceBridge Architecture

Direct Student Google Drive

When a student records, the audio is uploaded directly from their browser into their school Google Drive account under the VoiceBridge Recordings folder.

  • $0.00 Developer Operating Bill: Audio never touches developer cloud infrastructure.
  • Unlimited Minutes: No 3-minute recording caps or paid tier paywalls.
  • Least Privilege: Only accesses files created by VoiceBridge (drive.file scope).
  • District Retention: Files respect district Google Workspace archive & deletion rules.

Traditional Commercial Audio Vendors

Commercial recording platforms send student voice data to centralized corporate AWS/GCP servers.

  • Expensive recurring subscription licenses charged per student.
  • Student voice recordings stored in centralized third-party databases.
  • Arbitrary recording limits and restrictive monthly audio caps.
  • Data transfer and privacy risks with external commercial entities.

District IT Management

1-Click Google Workspace Force Deployment

Deploy VoiceBridge to thousands of student Chromebooks across your entire district in under 5 minutes.

1

Open Admin Console

Sign into admin.google.com as a Super Admin and navigate to Devices $ ightarrow$ Chrome $ ightarrow$ Apps & extensions.

2

Select Student OU

Select your target Organizational Unit (e.g. All Students or Special Education / IEP).

3

Force Install Extension

Click the + icon, select Add Chrome app or extension by ID, and set Installation policy to Force install.

4

Pre-Grant Microphone

Under Chrome Device Settings, add https://classroom.google.com to allowed audio capture URLs so students are never prompted with dialogs.
